# Ground Truth: Care Hours

**Care, measured in hours.**

An app in the Ground Truth series. Nursing homes report their staffing to the federal government through the Payroll Based Journal (PBJ) — actual payroll records, not surveys. Care Hours turns those reports into the plain number that matters: how many hours of care each resident actually gets per day.

## The seed data

- **Greenville, SC** — the 10 certified nursing homes with the most residents per day (of the 12 CMS lists for the city; together they cover 96% of the city's average daily residents).
- Staffing period: **PBJ 2026 Q2 (April 1 – June 30, 2026)**, from the CMS Care Compare "Provider Information" dataset, October 2026 refresh.
- For each home: total nurse hours per resident per day, RN hours, weekend staffing, star ratings, beds, and average residents — reproduced at the precision CMS publishes. Where CMS has no value, the app shows none. No third-party estimates, no review sites, nothing invented.

## How it works

Look up a nursing home and see its reported hours of care per resident per day — the number a family actually needs before choosing a home. No ads, no accounts, no selling your data.
